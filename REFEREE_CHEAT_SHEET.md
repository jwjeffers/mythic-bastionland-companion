# Mythic Bastionland — Referee Cheat Sheet

> **THE KNIGHT'S OATH:** *Seek the Myths • Honour the Seers • Protect the Realm*
> **PRIMACY OF ACTION (p. 16):** Past action taken by the players supersedes content generated by prompts or rules. Their deeds are reality.

---

## 1. The 5-Step Action Procedure (Page 16)
Whenever players attempt something outside combat, work down this list:
1. **Intent:** What are you trying to do?
2. **Leverage:** What makes it possible?
3. **Cost:** Does it take a full **Phase**, use a resource, cause **Virtue Loss**, or have a side-effect?
4. **Risk:** What's at risk? **No risk = no roll.** Otherwise, roll a **Save** (`d20 ≤ Virtue`) or a **Luck Roll** (`1d6`).
5. **Impact:** Show the consequences, honour the established risk, and move forward.

| Outcome | Choose One Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| **On a Success** | **Advance** (move in a good direction) • **Disrupt** (lessen a threat) • **Resolve** (put a problem to rest) |
| **On a Failure** | **Threaten** (create a new problem) • **Escalate** (make a problem worse) • **Execute** (deliver on a threat) |

### Virtues & Recovery (Pages 7, 9)
* **VIGOUR (VIG):** Strength, limbs, stamina. *Restored by:* **Sustenance** (feast) or hospitality. (`0 VIG` = **Slain**)
* **CLARITY (CLA):** Instinct, senses, lucid mind. *Restored by:* **Stimulant** (herbs) or Seer's guidance. (`0 CLA` = **Exposed**, attacks cannot be reduced by Guard/Armour)
* **SPIRIT (SPI):** Willpower, charm, fierce heart. *Restored by:* **Sacrament** (ritual), a **Monument**, or indulging your Knight's **Passion**. (`0 SPI` = **Broken**, all attacks Impaired to `d4`)
* *(Using a **Remedy** takes 1 full Phase, takes up a person/pack-beast's carrying capacity, and restores that Virtue for the **entire Company** present before resolving any Omens.)*

## 2. Daily Phases, Travel & Wilderness Rolls (Pages 17–19)

Each **Day** has **3 Phases**: **Morning** (Sunrise–Noon), **Afternoon** (Noon–Sunset), and **Night** (Sunset–Sunrise).

### Phase Actions & Travel Speeds
* **Trek:** Move **1 Hex** on foot or steed.
* **Gallop:** Move **2 Hexes** on a non-Exhausted steed (**steed loses `d6 VIG`**).
* **Cruise:** Move **3 Hexes** by boat (river/lake/sea) or steed on a rare paved road.
* **Search / Sweep:** Spend **1 Phase** to sweep a Hex, search for something known, or find a vantage point (grants overview of current Hex + general sense of adjacent Hexes).

### End-of-Phase & Road Hazards
* **Wilderness Roll:** Whenever ending a Phase in Wilderness (or camping outdoors at night, ignoring Landmarks), roll **`1d6`**:
  * **`1`:** Encounter the next **Omen** from a **random Myth** in this Realm *(or **City Quest** Omen if 12+ Glory)*.
  * **`2–3`:** Encounter the next **Omen** from the **nearest Myth**.
  * **`4–6`:** Encounter the Hex's **Landmark** *(if any; otherwise all clear)*.
* **Entering a Myth's Home Hex:** Ending a Phase in a Myth's Hex **automatically reveals its next Omen** without rolling, and the Knights get the drop on it when possible!
* **Night Travel/Exploration:** Costs every Knight **`d6 SPI`**. Without both a light and a guide, they are also **Travelling Blind**.
* **Missed Sleep:** Anyone who did not get proper sleep the previous day (e.g. hostile night Omen or Dire Weather) loses **`d6 CLA`** each morning.
* **Deprived Supplies:** Food/water/firewood are foraged automatically, but if deprived, each loses **`d6 VIG`** each morning.
* **Winter:** Camping outdoors or travelling at Night in Winter costs **`d6 VIG`**, plus check **Dire Weather** at the start of each Phase.

### Quick Referee `1d6` Tables (Pages 16, 18)
| Roll (`1d6`) | Luck Roll (p. 16) | Travelling Blind (p. 18) | Dire Weather (p. 18) | Local Mood in Holding (p. 18) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`1`** | **Crisis:** Immediately bad | **Circle back** to start | **Dire Weather:** Can't leave Hex or sleep | Occupied by a **looming/recent woe** |
| **`2–3`** | **Problem:** Potentially bad | **Drift** Left (`2`) or Right (`3`) | **Looming threat** (2nd in a row = Dire) | A sense of **things in decline** |
| **`4–6`** | **Blessing:** Welcome result | **Progress** as planned | **Fine weather** for travel | **Fine mood**, all seems well |

### Gathering Folklore: Who Knows What? (Page 19)
* **Vassals (Locals):** Know rumours about their **nearest Myth** + general direction (or exact hex if adjacent), plus Landmarks in their home and adjacent hexes.
* **Knights & Vagabonds:** Know rumours about a **random Myth** + rough direction, plus the nearest Landmark.
* **Seers:** Know the **rules, secrets, weaknesses, cures, and exact hexes of ALL Myths and Landmarks** in the Realm—but never give knowledge freely.

## 3. Combat, Feats, Gambits, Wounds & Scars (Pages 8–11)

### Core Combat Flow
1. **Surprise:** Players normally act first in any order. Unready characters must pass a **CLA Save** or miss the 1st turn.
2. **Turn Order:** Move, then perform an Action (always in that order). Changing weapons or dismounting is a free Minor Action.
3. **Attack Roll (No To-Hit!):** Gather all Attack Dice from your weapon + bonuses (`+d8` for upper hand/tactics) + multiple attackers teaming up on the same target. Roll all dice together:
   * Discard any dice showing **`4+`** to perform **Gambits** (or **`8+`** in melee for **Strong Gambits**).
   * Keep the **single highest remaining die** and subtract target **Armour (A1–A4)** = **Damage**.
   * *Impaired Attacks* (unarmed, Broken, Exhausted, Long weapon in tight space) roll **`d4`** regardless of normal dice.
   * *Blast Attacks* affect a whole area or group, rolling **separately** against each individual target.

### The 3 Knightly Feats (Page 10)
Each Feat can be used once per Attack by each combatant. Afterward, pass the Save or become **Fatigued** (cannot use Feats until rested):
* **SMITE (Before rolling melee Attack):** Gain **`+d12`** OR **`Blast`**. Then pass a **VIG Save** or become *Fatigued*.
* **FOCUS (After rolling Attack):** Perform **1 Gambit** without discarding a die. Then pass a **CLA Save** or become *Fatigued*.
* **DENY (After an Attack roll vs you or ally in arm's reach):** Discard **1 Attack die** from the enemy's roll. Then pass a **SPI Save** or become *Fatigued*.

### Gambits (Discard a `4+` Attack Die — Page 10)
Target gets a **VIG Save** to ignore (except *Bolster* and *Move*):
* **Bolster:** `+1` total Damage *(no Save)*
* **Move:** Move after the Attack *(no Save)*
* **Repel:** Push foe away from you
* **Stop:** Prevent foe from moving next turn
* **Impair:** Foe's weapon is Impaired (`d4`) next turn
* **Trap:** Trap foe's shield until your next turn
* **Dismount:** Knock foe off steed (causes `+d6` Damage)
* **Strong Gambit (`8+` die in melee):** Choose **No Save allowed** OR **Greater Effect** (disarm item, break wooden shield/weapon, remove helm).

### Damage, Wounds, Scars & Morale (Pages 9–10)
* **Guard (GD):** Subtract Damage from Guard first. Guard fully recovers after resting post-combat.
* **Exact `0 GD` = SCAR!** If Damage reduces you to **exact `0 GD`** with no leftover Damage to VIG, roll the die that caused it on the **Scar Table**:

| Die Roll | Scar | Effect & Guard Growth |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`1`** | **Distress** | A lucky escape. Lose `d6 SPI`. |
| **`2`** | **Disfigurement** | Permanent mark (`1`:Eye, `2`:Cheek, `3`:Neck, `4`:Torso, `5`:Nose, `6`:Jaw). If max GD ≤ 2, `+d6` max GD. |
| **`3`** | **Smash** | Sudden spray of blood. Lose `d6 VIG`. |
| **`4`** | **Stun** | Pain drowns senses. Lose `d6 CLA`. If max GD ≤ 4, `+d6` max GD. |
| **`5`** | **Rupture** | Innards pierced. Lose `2d6 VIG`. |
| **`6`** | **Gouge** | Flesh torn from bone. When stitched up, if max GD ≤ 6, `+d6` max GD. |
| **`7`** | **Concussion** | Heavy blow numbs mind. Lose `2d6 CLA`. |
| **`8`** | **Tear** | Taken in struggle (`1`:Nose, `2`:Ear, `3`:Finger, `4`:Thumb, `5`:Eye, `6`:Scalp). When patched, if max GD ≤ 8, `+d6` max GD. |
| **`9`** | **Agony** | Torturous break. Lose `2d6 SPI`. |
| **`10`** | **Mutilation** | Limb lost/useless (`1-2`:Leg, `3-4`:Shield Arm, `5-6`:Sword Arm). By next Season if max GD ≤ 10, `+d6` max GD. |
| **`11`** | **Doom** | Cheated death haunts you. If you take a Mortal Wound this Season you are **Slain** instead. |
| **`12`** | **Humiliation** | A most dolorous stroke. When you achieve revenge, if max GD ≤ 12, `+d6` max GD. |

* **Leftover Damage to VIG = WOUND!** Must pass a **VIG Save**:
  * **Pass:** Keep fighting. If you lost **half or more** of your remaining VIG, become **Exhausted** (attacks Impaired until full night's rest).
  * **Fail = Mortal Wound:** Out of the action, dying within an hour unless tended to.
  * **`0 VIG` = Slain:** Instant death.
* **Wavering Morale (NPCs only):** Individuals who take a Wound, or groups who lose half their number, must pass a **SPI Save** or rout/surrender.

## 4. Glory, Seasons, Ages & Domain Rulership (Pages 6, 17, 20–21)

### Glory & Ranks
* **+1 Glory:** Resolve a Myth, win a staked duel/joust, win a grand tournament, win a historic battle, or enter a new Age.
* **Ranks:** `0` **Knight-Errant** (Lead Warband) • `3` **Knight-Gallant** (Council/Court) • `6` **Knight-Tenant** (Rule Holding) • `9` **Knight-Dominant** (Rule Seat of Power) • `12` **Knight-Radiant** (**The City Quest**, pp. 172–173).

### Advancing Time & Domain Rolls (`1d6`)
* **Between Seasons:** Restore all Virtues + pick **Pilgrimage** (learn unknown part of Realm), **Courtesy** (contact/favour), or **Service** (small improvement). Replace any resolved Myths with new Myths.
* **Between Ages:** Restore Virtues, **+1 Glory**, reroll Virtues on `d12+d6` (keep higher if **Mature**, keep lower if **Old**; Old also loses `d12 VIG` per Age) + pick **Duty**, **Succession**, or **Legacy**.
* **Domain Crisis Roll (Start of Season / Return):** `1` **Calamity** (2 Crises), `2–3` **Dilemma** (Choose 1 of 2 Crises), `4–6` **Prosperity**.
  * **The 6 Crises:** `1` Chaos (Justice), `2` Debt (Coin), `3` Famine (Food), `4` Misery (Spirits), `5` Panic (Safety), `6` Doubt (Authority). *(3+ unresolved Crises at end of Season = **Misrule**!)*
