Feats, Gambits, Wounds & Scars
pp. 9–10
Smite (VIG Save)
Before melee roll: +d12 or Blast. Fail = Fatigued.
Focus (CLA Save)
After roll: 1 free Gambit (keep die). Fail = Fatigued.
Deny (SPI Save)
Enemy discards 1 Attack die. Fail = Fatigued.
Gambits (Discard 4+ Die)

Foe gets VIG Save (except Bolster/Move). 8+ melee = Strong (No Save OR disarm / break shield / remove helm).

  • Bolster: +1 Damage (no Save)
  • Move: Move after Attack (no Save)
  • Repel: Push foe away
  • Stop: Foe can't move next turn
  • Impair: Foe weapon d4 next turn
  • Trap: Trap shield until next turn
  • Dismount: Unhorse (+d6 Damage)
Damage, Scars & Wounds
  • Subtract from Guard (GD) first.
  • Exact 0 GD: Roll the attacking die on the Scar Table.
  • VIG Damage = Wound! Pass VIG Save:
    • Pass: Fight on (if ≥ ½ VIG lost → Exhausted, d4 attacks).
    • Fail = Mortal Wound: Helpless, dies in 1 hr unless tended.
    • 0 VIG = Slain!
